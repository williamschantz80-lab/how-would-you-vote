Texas House votes · 2019–2025

How would you have voted?

Here are 18 real bills the Texas House voted on between 2019 and 2025.

For each one you'll see when it was voted on, what the bill did, a plain-English version, and what each side argued. Then tell us how you would have voted: Yes or No.

There are no right or wrong answers. Just vote the way you would if you were a lawmaker. You'll see your results at the end.
